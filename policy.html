Estimate & Communications Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy explains how website inquiries, estimates, scheduling, and communications with Crawl Space Pros are handled.

1. Free estimate requests

Submitting the website form, calling, or texting is a request to discuss your project. It does not create a service contract or guarantee availability.

2. Preliminary website information

A problem selected on the website, a photo, description, or phone discussion may help us understand what you are seeing, but it is not a final diagnosis.

3. Estimates and project scope

An estimate is governed by the scope, assumptions, price, validity period, and other terms stated in the estimate itself.

4. Scheduling

Requested dates or timeframes are not confirmed until Crawl Space Pros specifically confirms them.

5. Calls, texts, and email

By providing contact information in connection with your inquiry, you authorize us to use it to respond about the requested estimate, scheduling, inspection, or service.
