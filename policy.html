CUSTOMER POLICY

Estimate & Communications Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Estimate & Communications Policy explains how website inquiries, estimates, scheduling, and communications with Crawl Space Pros are handled. It supplements, and does not replace, any written estimate, work authorization, service agreement, or other project-specific document.

1. Free estimate requests

Submitting the website form, calling, or texting us is a request to discuss your project. It does not create a service contract, reserve a date, guarantee availability, or obligate either you or Crawl Space Pros to proceed with work.

2. Preliminary website information

A problem selected on the website, a photo, description, or phone discussion may help us understand what you are seeing, but it is not a final diagnosis. Crawl space conditions can be hidden or change from one part of a structure to another. Recommendations and pricing may depend on an inspection and the conditions actually observed.

3. Estimates and project scope

An estimate is governed by the scope, assumptions, price, validity period, and other terms stated in the estimate itself. Conditions that were concealed, inaccessible, inaccurately described, or discovered after work begins may require a revised scope, additional authorization, or a separate estimate.

4. Scheduling

Requested dates or timeframes are not confirmed until Crawl Space Pros specifically confirms them. Scheduling may be affected by access, weather, material availability, existing site conditions, emergencies, or other work.

5. Calls, texts, and email

By giving us contact information in connection with your inquiry, you authorize us to use that information to respond to the inquiry and communicate about the requested estimate, scheduling, inspection, or service. Standard carrier message or data charges may apply to text messages. An estimate request is not, by itself, consent to receive unrelated automated marketing messages.

6. Service area

The website describes our general service area in Southwest Washington. Availability can vary by project location and workload. Contact us if you want to confirm whether we currently serve a specific address.

7. Photos and website examples

Project and crawl space photos on the website illustrate conditions that may be encountered in the field. Every property is different. A photo does not promise that another property has the same condition, requires the same repair, or will have an identical result.

8. Emergencies

The website form is not monitored as an emergency service. If a condition presents an immediate threat to people or property, use the appropriate emergency or utility contact rather than relying on the website form.

9. Questions

For a question about an estimate, schedule, or communication, call or text Crawl Space Pros at (360) 470-4956.
