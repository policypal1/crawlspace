COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you schedule the cleanout.

What does a crawl-space cleanout include?+

The exact scope depends on what is under the home. Cleanout work can include removal of old insulation, vapor barrier, rodent or nesting debris, construction waste, and damaged materials, followed by hauling and disposal.

Do I need to know whether my insulation should be replaced?+

No. We inspect the crawl space first and explain the condition of the materials. You do not have to diagnose the problem before requesting an estimate.

Are you going to try to sell me a full encapsulation?+

The purpose of the inspection is to see what is actually happening underneath the home. We explain what we find and what options make sense for the space. You decide what work you want to approve.

Can you clean up after rodents?+

Yes, crawl-space cleanouts can include removal of rodent droppings, nesting debris, contaminated materials, and damaged insulation. If an active pest problem needs separate treatment, we can explain that as part of the inspection.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. Request an estimate online or call/text (360) 470-4956 to schedule an inspection.
