Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. Crawl Space Pros offers free estimates so you can understand the scope of the work before deciding what to do next.

What crawl space services do you provide?+

Services include crawlspace drain systems, vapor barriers, insulation, post and beam repairs, French drains, encapsulation, and moisture or water intrusion solutions.

How do I know which repair I actually need?+

You do not need to diagnose it yourself. Start with what you are noticing, then the crawl space can be inspected to determine the cause and the appropriate repair.

Can you help with standing water or drainage problems?+

Yes. Drainage and moisture intrusion are part of the services Crawl Space Pros handles, including crawlspace drain systems and French drains where appropriate.

Do you replace damaged insulation and vapor barriers?+

Yes. Insulation and vapor barrier work are both core services.

What areas do you serve?+

Crawl Space Pros serves homeowners in Southwest Washington, including the Castle Rock and Camas areas.
