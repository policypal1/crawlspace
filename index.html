Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. We can take a look at the crawl space and give you an estimate before you decide how you want to move forward.

What crawl space services do you provide?+

We handle crawlspace drain systems, vapor barriers, insulation, post and beam repairs, French drains, encapsulation, and moisture or water intrusion problems.

How do I know which repair I actually need?+

You do not need to figure that out yourself. Tell us what you have noticed and we can inspect the crawl space to find the cause.

Can you help with standing water or drainage problems?+

Yes. We work on drainage and moisture problems, including crawlspace drain systems and French drains when they are the right fit for the property.

Do you replace damaged insulation and vapor barriers?+

Yes. Insulation and vapor barrier work are both services we regularly handle.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve homeowners across Southwest Washington, including Castle Rock, Camas, Longview, Kelso, Vancouver, and nearby communities.
