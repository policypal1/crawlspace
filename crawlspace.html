Crawl Space Cleanout Questions.

What does a crawl space cleanout include?+

The exact scope depends on what is under your home. Cleanouts can include removing old or damaged insulation, old vapor barrier, rodent debris, construction debris, and other unwanted material from the crawl space.

Do you haul the old material away?+

Yes. The goal of the cleanout is to remove the unwanted material from underneath the home and haul it away as part of the agreed scope.

Do I need to know exactly what needs removed?+

No. We can inspect the crawl space, show you what we found, and explain what should be included in the cleanout before you decide how to move forward.

Can you remove old insulation and vapor barrier?+

Yes. Old insulation and vapor barrier removal are common parts of crawl space cleanout work.

Do you offer free estimates?+

Yes. We can inspect the crawl space and give you an estimate before you decide whether to move forward.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve homeowners across Southwest Washington, including Longview, Kelso, Castle Rock, Camas, Vancouver, and nearby communities.
