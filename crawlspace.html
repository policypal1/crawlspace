Questions before you request an estimate.

How much does a crawl space cleanout cost?+

It depends on the size of the crawl space, access, and what needs to be removed. We inspect first and give you a straightforward estimate before you decide whether to move forward.

Are you going to try to sell me a bunch of extra work?+

The estimate starts with what you asked us to look at and what we find underneath the home. If we notice something else that deserves attention, we can explain it, but you decide what work you want done.

What does a crawl space cleanout include?+

The exact scope depends on your crawl space. Cleanouts can include removing old or damaged insulation, old vapor barrier, rodent debris, construction debris, and other unwanted material.

Do you haul the old material away?+

Yes. The agreed cleanout scope includes getting the unwanted material out from underneath the home and hauling it away.

Do I need to know exactly what needs removed?+

No. Tell us what you noticed. We can inspect the crawl space, show you what we found, and define the cleanout scope before you decide.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve homeowners across Southwest Washington, including Longview, Kelso, Castle Rock, Camas, Vancouver, and nearby communities.
