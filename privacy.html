Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crawl Space Pros (“Crawl Space Pros,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website, request an estimate, or contact us about crawl space services.

1. Information you provide

If the website estimate form is enabled and you submit it, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, the type of crawl space issue you are experiencing, and any message you send. We also receive information you provide when you call, text, or otherwise communicate with us.

2. Information received automatically

Our website host and internet infrastructure providers may automatically receive technical information that is normally transmitted when a website is used, such as an IP address, browser or device type, requested pages, and date and time information. We use this information only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and understand use of the website.

3. Embedded maps and third-party services

The website may display embedded Google Maps or other third-party content. When you load or interact with third-party content, that provider may receive information from your browser or device under its own privacy terms. We do not control a third party’s independent data practices.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to estimate requests and service inquiries.
  • To communicate about scheduling, inspections, estimates, and requested work.
  • To provide customer service and maintain business records.
  • To operate, maintain, protect, and improve the website and our services.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse, or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

5. How we disclose information

We may provide information to vendors that perform services for us, such as website hosting, form or email delivery, communications, data storage, or similar business-support functions. We may also disclose information to professional advisers, insurers, government authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to protect rights, comply with law, or address a legal or security matter. If the business is reorganized, sold, or transferred, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not knowingly sell personal information for monetary consideration. We do not treat a request for an estimate as consent to unrelated automated marketing.

6. Data minimization and retention

We aim to collect only information reasonably useful for the purposes described above. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the inquiry or business relationship, recordkeeping, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, fraud-prevention, or security purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No website, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit information through the website and may contact us by phone or text instead. You may also contact us to ask about information you have provided, or to request a reasonable correction or deletion. We may retain information when reasonably necessary or required by law.

9. Children

This website is intended for people seeking residential or property services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, service providers, or practices change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version.
