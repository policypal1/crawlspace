Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Crawl Space Pros is committed to making information about our services and estimate process reasonably accessible to people with disabilities.

Our accessibility approach

We aim to use WCAG 2.1 Level AA as a practical reference when maintaining and improving the site.

Measures we work to support

  • Clear page structure, headings, labels, and link text.
  • Keyboard access to interactive controls where reasonably possible.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful website images.
  • Readable text contrast and responsive layouts.
  • Form fields with visible labels and understandable instructions.
